Use after reviewing and understanding the implementation. These are independent research claims, not employer, live trading or issuer-NAV replication results.

- **Engineered an issuer-grounded defined-outcome research engine**, translating public buffer/cap terms into signed European claims and evaluating 36 fixed entry-time/spot/volatility states, with explicit funding costs, fee conventions and integer-account settlement.
- **Validated payoff and valuation logic with 24 automated tests**, including independent risk-neutral integration, put-call parity, strike boundaries and cash conservation; separated model marks from typed, timestamp-checked NAV/bid-ask inputs and documented missing execution evidence.

Evidence: [protocol](../protocol.json), [full results and five figures](../reports/demo/REPORT.md), [tests](../tests/test_research.py) and [source ledger](../defined_outcome_research/data/issuer_terms.json). Counts refer to this fixed study and test suite, not transactions or a private production system.

For trading/derivatives roles, emphasize pricing, remaining-period payoff and account constraints. For investment/product roles, emphasize source interpretation, fee definitions and scenario communication. For DS roles this is complementary numerical/data-quality evidence; the real-data text and financial-ML studies are stronger primary links. Do not describe a hypothetical scenario return as achieved P&L or reuse prior private internship basis-point figures.
